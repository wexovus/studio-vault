# Sparta helots reel — production notes

The first fully-$0 video: local TTS ([[local-tts-voicebox-kokoro-2026-09-29]]) + drawn renderer.

## Research discipline that worked
- Two research subagents (society evolution / helot wars) stalled: one silent 25+ min, the other
  crawling through primary sources and hitting a DuckDuckGo bot-wall. Stopped both rather than
  waiting; the producer's own targeted verification against named sources (Perseus Plutarch Lyc. 28,
  lexundria Herodotus 9.28, How-Wells commentary, Armijo Nature paper on the 464 BC quake,
  Aristotle Politics 1269b on women's land) produced the fact file in minutes. Targeted
  verification beats broad delegation for short-form: every script beat needs 1-3 sources, not a survey.
- Fact file: `/opt/data/cache/scratch/sparta_facts_verified.md` (not in the vault; it is project data).
- The contested claim (krypteia: Plutarch's murder version vs Xenophon's rebels version) is narrated
  AS contested in the script — "Plutarch says... Xenophon says... pick your horror." Presenting the
  disagreement is both more accurate and more interesting than picking a side.

## Production facts
- Script: 10 beats, 367 words, hook-payoff pairs; [[humanizer]] pass applied (no em-dash spam,
  no "it's not just X, it's Y" framing, numbers stated plainly).
- VO: am_michael (Kokoro) → 162.3s. Timeline built from real duration, word-weighted captions
  (357 tokens, punctuation attached per the vertical-reel skill).
- 20 shots / 10 beats (establishing + close per beat), stages: storm, countryside, map, plain,
  night, crowd, sky. Pixel audit: 12 frames, all pass (captions present, figures visible on every
  stage including night/storm).
- Master: `/mnt/bulk/studio-media/artifacts/sparta_helots.mp4` (2:42, 1080x1920, 52.6 MB).
  Runtime accepted at 162s (Nicolas's call) though the reel plan said ~85s — history beats with
  two subjects (society evolution + a war) need the room.

## What to do next time
- When a produce uses free/local components, report the $0 explicitly — the bible budget is the
  reference point and a $0 run against a $9 budget is the headline, not the runtime.
- 136 wpm means ~2.3 words per second of planned runtime; budget scripts from the synthesized
  duration, and for history topics assume the topic will outgrow the 85s plan (this one was 1.9x).
