## PRODUCTION RULES (evidence-backed)

1. **Interval structure = Pillow diagram plate** (house decision 0001). Never generate "people walking fast then slow" — concept line, wrong-subject bait.
2. **Mixed media:** generated hero (senior walker, morning light, lifestyle_natural) + Pexels walking B-roll + diagram plate + title/end cards. One grade across all four sources (decision 0004).
3. **Pacing:** two species of hook exist in the niche — explainer promise-stacking (ours) vs follow-along instant-start. Ours must hit content in ≤15s.
4. **Language register:** short spoken sentences. "You" for stakes, "we" for effort. Jargon always translated ("aerobic capacity — that's your engine").
5. **Taboos (niche-wide, never violate):** no fear appeals, no equipment gatekeeping, no speed pressure, no mid-video CTA, no medical claims, no condescension.
6. **Comment keyword:** WALK (mirrors Dan Go's proven mechanic — feeds lead capture for the product funnel).
